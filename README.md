# KML Route Video Maker

**Open the app: https://oceanfarm1992-design.github.io/kml-video-maker/**

Turn a KML route into a map video in real time. The camera follows the route over
satellite imagery, zooms in at stops and turns, and the video is recorded straight
from your browser. Your KML file never leaves your computer.

## How to use

1. Open the app in Chrome or Edge.
2. Drop a `.kml` or `.kmz` file onto the page (exported from Google Earth or Google My Maps).
3. Press **Preview** to watch, or **Record video** to make a file. Recordings save to
   your Downloads folder automatically (MP4 in Chrome/Edge, otherwise WebM).

Keep the tab visible while recording, since browsers slow down hidden tabs.

## Features

- Follow camera with 3D tilt that turns with the direction of travel
- Zooms in and slows down at stops and sharp turns, zooms out on open stretches
- Overview at the start and end of the route
- Stop pins and names, arrival pop-ups, distance and progress bar
- English road names for the roads on the route
- Background music made in the browser (energetic dance, upbeat pop, cinematic) or your own audio file, recorded into the video
- Sizes: 1920×1080, 1280×720, 1080×1920 (vertical), 1080×1080
